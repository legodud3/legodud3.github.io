What is love?

January 31, 2026·1 min read

Self-preservation is an axiom of humans. Love is when you willingly override the self-preservation instinct for the preservation of someone or something else. While there are different types of love, they all share this characteristic.

P.S. Maybe that's why the song goes, "...baby don't hurt me, no more."
