# Time

Is time absolute or relative? An hour is not the same for your brain, though it may be for the clock. An hour with a friend? Doing chores? Writing? Meeting at work? Traveling? Stuck in traffic? The first hour with a newborn? An hour in Paris? All of these are different to your mind because of the volume, nature, or relevance of the memories that hour creates. 

The mind seems to track "meaning," which then has a perceived value (gain vs loss) that fluctuates depending on the event that transpired.
