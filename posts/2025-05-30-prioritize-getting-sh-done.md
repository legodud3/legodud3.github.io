# Prioritize Getting Sh*t Done

Finally started putting my daily writings online. It's been approximately two months of daily habits. Feel better writing that I created something for the day. Before the first word is put down, however, it's mostly bad. So now the goal is to be a simple three-step:
1. Write nearly every day.
2. Write with a pen & paper, not a computer.
3. A few times a week, consolidate & put up writing online so it's archived forever.
