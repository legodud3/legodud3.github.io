I am tempted to pickup an xteink device. xteink looked at a mature market (ereaders) and had a unique set of insights about that market:- Miniatures: Miniature size not miniature features. Particularly pronounced in watches which for a long time have been ridiculously large compared to most wrists. Now its about right sized and even undersized dials with no feature compromise. Trend also visible in mini / micro handbags
- Kindles are just shy of portable: Kindles are small devices (vs tablets) but not everyone likes carrying a Kindle around. Because you already have a phone, a laptop, and in many cases a tablet. What's the justification for a 3rd / 4th device?
- Projection as 'a reader': Consumers (well, Kindle consumers atleast) love to project themselves as readers. And tell the world about it. Sticking an xteink on the back of a phone -- ah yes, an unused piece of real estate that everyone can see -- hits right
- Projection as 'not addicted to my phone': In addition to projecting themselves as readers, Kindle consumers also want to be perceived as not having a screen time addiction. So voila, you can carry your slightly-larger-than-wallet sized xteink and read everywhere.

Xteink will likely enjoy a brief hype cycle driven by social media vanity, but the long-term unit economics are going to be brutal. Hardware margins are famously paper thin, and unlike Amazon—which uses cheap Kindles as a customer acquisition hook to sell books—Xteink has no content ecosystem to monetize because they are insisting users bring their own books. And most importantly, is the long term barrier to reading device size?
