---
title: Nearly empty gyms
date: 2026-01-25
tag: Reflection
---

I love working out in nearly empty gyms, which I define as gyms that are operating at <20% but >0% capacity. I find I can lift more weights and get done quicker. I'm sure both of these are physically possible even if the gym is at 60% capacity. But I like it when I see a nearly empty gym.

And so in the grand tradition of generalization, my hypothesis is that I function best in general in nearly empty gym like environments. There should be SOMETHING going on in the arena. A thought or memory I've held on to, some not crazy background activity (like a dishwasher), some light not overstimulating music. I will try creating as many hours of the day as I can to be like nearly empty gyms.
