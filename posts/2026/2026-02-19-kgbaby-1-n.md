A web browser based audio only baby monitor where one device functions as a broadcast unit (chid) and the other as a receiver unit (parent). Implemented the project with the aid of my three AI interns: Codex+Gemini CLI+VSCode. Hosted on GitHub Pages. Plan to do lots of real world testing over an upcoming weekend trip where we won't have access to our trusty Motorola AM21.

Developed a better understanding of building software products with AI:

1. [Not nuanced] It is still amazing that you can just think of things and make them yourself in a few hours.

2. Testing in the real world improves a product, not thinking about it. And for what it's worth, AI can't test a product in the real world. I can. You can.

3. Testing reveals where stuff breaks. And that - coding for the edge cases & for reliability & for robustness - is as important as the core functionality or the UI/UX.

4. Just hit go! It will never be perfect. So I posted about it on ShowHN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47072918
