Following on from my previous [post](https://legodud3.github.io/view.html?post=2026%2F2026-02-19-kgbaby-1-n.md), I took KGBaby on the road to our weekend vacation rental: Shoutout [Honge on the rocks](https://www.instagram.com/honge.on.the.rocks/?hl=en). And it failed. Spectacularly. 

I was, of course, very excited to try it out in the 'real world'. I had assumed some hiccups but not a complete failure. Given it was a weekend vacation, I did not spend time debugging or improving it much. Once I came back, I was at it with a vengeance. After some digging around, I found out what I believe is the issue and implemented a solution. KGBaby worked flawlessly on my home Deco mesh, but failed on a random Airbnb Wi‑Fi even with both devices on the same network.
Root cause was probably network policy, whereby direct WebRTC couldn’t establish a reliable peer-to-peer path.I added TURN relay fallback, but compromised privacy in the process. We live to try another day!

What I learnt: You build a better product by testing and breaking it.
