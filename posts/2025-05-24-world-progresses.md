# The World Progresses Because...

The world progresses because humans want more, we are evolutionary wired to never be content with what we have. My guess is that this is because the human brain is always:
a) Optimizing for newer & different experiences.
b) Trying to devise against unknown & known risks, including real events (in fact, beyond a particular vector of money, food, shelter, relationships), all risks are tail risks.
c) Extremely susceptible to the sunk cost fallacy of protecting what one has.

The world around us evolves everything just to make sure it fulfills the need of "humans wanting more."
