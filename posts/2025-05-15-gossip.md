# Gossip

Gossip—either narrating stories or opinions—about other people is widespread & shared with approximately 100% of the world doing it. Great PMF. Why do we do it? Mostly because it makes us feel better about ourselves, establishes an approach/opinion as "opinion," gives us validation. 
* **What is the point of Gossip?** (May 16, 2025) To make the reader go through the same experience as the narrator. 
* **What do you do?** How do you react when your worldview or a widely held & believed to be true notion is shattered? 
* **Which of my beliefs would be like this?** I qualify for a similar level of shock?
