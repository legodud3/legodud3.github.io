# Gossip

Gossip—whether narrating stories or opinions—about other people is widespread and shared by approximately 100% of the world. It has great product-market fit. Why do we do it? Mostly because it makes us feel better about ourselves, establishes an approach or opinion as valid, and provides us with validation. 
* **What is the point of Gossip?** (May 16, 2025) To make the reader go through the same experience as the narrator. 
* **What do you do?** How do you react when your worldview or a widely held & believed to be true notion is shattered? 
* **Which of my beliefs would be like this?** I qualify for a similar level of shock?
