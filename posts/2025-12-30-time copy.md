# Time

Time = absolute or relative? An hour is not an hour for your brain, though it may be for the clock. An hour with a friend? Doing chores? Writing? Meeting @ work? Travel? Traffic? The first hour with a newborn? An hour in Paris? All different to your mind because of the volume, nature, or relevance of the memories that hour creates. 

The mind seems to track "meaning," which then has a perceived value (gain vs loss) that fluctuates depending on the event that transpired.
