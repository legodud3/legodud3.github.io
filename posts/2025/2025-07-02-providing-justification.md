# Providing Justification

I have begun internalizing that I don't need to give a reason for my thoughts or actions if I don't want to, and I don't owe anyone an explanation. 

Because this implies that I have the freedom to determine what is private to me and why, situations when an explanation or rationale undermines my own objectives, and who deserves an explanation versus not. I believe who you choose to share your why with for which situations is an instrument I have authority over. It's a gradual process to recognize this vs 'answer by default'. Baby steps!
