# Prioritize Getting Sh*t Done

I finally started putting my daily writings online. It's been approximately two months of daily habits. I feel better knowing that I created something for the day. However, before the first word is put down, it's mostly bad. So now the goal is a simple three-step process:
1. Write nearly every day.
2. Write with a pen and paper, not a computer.
3. A few times a week, consolidate and publish writing online so it's archived forever.
