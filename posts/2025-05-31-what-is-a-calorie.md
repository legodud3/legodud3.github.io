# What is a Calorie?

We all become conscious of food labels & read the calories. As much as energy is measured in kilocalories (or what we call "calories" quite mistakenly). The average male is 2000-2500 kcal per day. But what does that mean? 
$1 \text{ kcal} = 1000 \text{ calories}$. 
Enough to keep your phone charged—FULLY CHARGED—for 10 days! 
Enough to boil 20L of ice-cold water. 
Equivalent to the energy released by $40 \text{ hand grenades}$. 
Enough to put a $1kg$ satellite into low-earth orbit via a typical Electric Vehicle. 

In essence, the human body on average has about 100,000 kcal of stored energy... 50 days of "reserve power." You can use the above equivalencies to visualize how much work the human body can actually do.
