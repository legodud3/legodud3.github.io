# What is a Calorie?

We all become conscious of food labels and read the calories. Energy is measured in kilocalories (or what we mistakenly call "calories"). The average male requires 2000-2500 kcal per day. But what does that mean? 
$1 \text{ kcal} = 1000 \text{ calories}$. 
This is enough to keep your phone charged—FULLY CHARGED—for 10 days! 
It's enough to boil 20L of ice-cold water. 
Equivalent to the energy released by $40 \text{ hand grenades}$. 
It's enough to put a $1kg$ satellite into low-earth orbit via a typical Electric Vehicle. 

In essence, the human body, on average, has about 100,000 kcal of stored energy... 50 days of "reserve power." You can use the above equivalencies to visualize how much work the human body can actually do.
