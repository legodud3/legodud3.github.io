# Habit Retention

Writing after ~3 months. I forgot about writing for many days and never actually did it. It's a brutal reminder that habits have terrible retention curves unless there is an external stimulus or external incentive, even when you are fully convinced and tell yourself that there are benefits from the activity. 

The path to self-improvement is hard—gym, writing, speaking publicly, learning an instrument, learning a skill (say, coding), reading, etc. Being distracted adults who convince ourselves that other things are more important doesn't help. 

I didn't write because... xyz? No, you didn't write because you (I mean I) didn't make it a priority. However, this is what makes us human. We can reflect over long periods of time, and we can group. But we can also come back to the "good habit." And I will try again!
