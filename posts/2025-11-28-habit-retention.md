# Habit Retention

Writing after ~3 months. I forgot many days about it but never actually did it. Brutal reminder on the fact that habits are habits—have terrible retention curves unless there is external stimulus or external incentives. Even when you are fully convinced & tell yourself that there are benefits from the activity. 

The way to self-improvement is hard—gym, writing, speaking publicly, learning an instrument, learning a skill (say, coding), reading etc. vs being distracted adults who essentially convince ourselves that other things are more important doesn't help. 

I wrote because... xyz? No, you didn't write because you (I mean I) didn't make it a priority. However, this is what makes us human. We can reflect over long periods of time and we can group. But we can also come back to the "good habit." And I will try again!
