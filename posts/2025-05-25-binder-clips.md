# Binder Clips

One of the most well-designed everyday objects. Does one thing really well—hold loose paper together. It's obvious how to use it. It holds beautiful simple spring mechanism, means no maintenance & minimal chance of breakage. Can also be used to hold the sheets in your hand. Easy to add or remove sheets. Doesn't damage the paper (much). Doesn't obstruct reading or writing. What's not to like? Did I mention it looks great?
