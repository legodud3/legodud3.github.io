# Colonies

NW loves using colonies to extend power as opposed to a full-fledged war. Why? Start with Low-Cost Hypothesis: 
a) Colonies don't affect target completely.
b) Low cost vs full-blown deployment.
c) Reversible / easy to shut shop on colony.
d) Info gain: To decide on full acquisition.

PS: My role (corporate strategy) is effectively that of a colonist!
