# Colonies

NW loves using colonies to extend power as opposed to engaging in a full-fledged war. Why? Start with the Low-Cost Hypothesis: 
a) Colonies don't completely affect the target.
b) They are low cost compared to full-blown deployment.
c) They are reversible and easy to shut down.
d) Information gain: To decide on full acquisition.

P.S. My role (corporate strategy) is effectively that of a colonist!
