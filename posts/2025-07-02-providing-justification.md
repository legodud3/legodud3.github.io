# Providing Justification

Have begun internalizing that I don't need to give a reason for my thoughts or actions if I don't want to and (b) don't owe anyone an explanation. 

Because (a) and (b) imply that I have the freedom of determining what is private to me & why, when an explanation or rationale undermines my own objectives, and who deserves an explanation versus not. And that's an instrument about how people perceive me / my thinking. Gradual process. Babysteps!
