# Providing Justification

I have begun internalizing that I don't need to give a reason for my thoughts or actions if I don't want to, and I don't owe anyone an explanation. 

Because this implies that I have the freedom to determine what is private to me and why, especially when an explanation or rationale undermines my own objectives, and who deserves an explanation versus not. This is an instrument about how people perceive me and my thinking. It's a gradual process. Baby steps!
